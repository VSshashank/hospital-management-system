Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about HealthConnect

Yes, HealthConnect is fully HIPAA compliant. We employ end-to-end encryption, secure data storage, and strict access controls to ensure all protected health information (PHI) is handled in accordance with HIPAA regulations. Our platform undergoes regular security audits and maintains comprehensive documentation for compliance verification.

HealthConnect integrates with all major EHR systems including Epic, Cerner, Allscripts, and athenahealth. Our flexible API allows for seamless data exchange regardless of your current system. We also support HL7 FHIR standards for interoperability with emerging healthcare technologies.

We offer flexible pricing based on your organization's size and needs. For individual providers, we have a monthly subscription model. For larger healthcare organizations, we offer enterprise licensing with volume discounts. Patients can access basic features for free, with premium features available through a low monthly subscription. Contact our sales team for a customized quote.

Your data is protected with multiple layers of security including end-to-end encryption, blockchain-based audit trails, and role-based access controls. We never sell your data to third parties and adhere to strict data minimization principles. All data is stored in SOC 2 compliant data centers with 24/7 monitoring and regular penetration testing.
